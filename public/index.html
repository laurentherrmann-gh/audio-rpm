How the numbers relate

A 4-stroke engine fires each cylinder once every two crank turns, so the loudest tone is the firing frequency = rpm / 60 × cylinders / 2 (4 cylinders at 6,000 rpm → 200 Hz). Both detectors look for that tone in the band set by idle and redline, then convert back to rpm. If a detector reads exactly half or double, it locked onto a half order or a harmonic: fix it with Calibrate (type the in-game rpm while holding steady revs) or by changing the cylinder count. With the simulated engine, the error lines under each reading compare against the true rpm over the last 20 s.
