Settings

Firing frequency = rpm / 60 × cylinders / 2 (4 cylinders at 6,000 rpm → 200 Hz). Auto cylinders: no two cylinders fire exactly alike, so the sound also repeats once per engine cycle; the faint tones this adds between the firing harmonics are counted while the revs are steady (idle or cruising). Auto · rally only considers 4, 5, 6 and 8 cylinders (every DiRT Rally 2.0 car): fewer ways to be wrong and a narrower search, but a V10/V12 would be miscounted, so use Auto · any for those. Exactly half or double the real rpm means a wrong lock: pick the cylinder count by hand, or Calibrate (type the in-game rpm while holding steady revs). Measured lag needs a known truth: the simulated engine, or the loopback test for the real capture path.
